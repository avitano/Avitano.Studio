About Me

Product design leadership, hands-on.

My job is to make the designers on my team successful, and that is how I measure myself. So they present their own work, and the bank's C-level asks them questions directly.

Embedded in five Tribes

Seven designers across Loans, Credit Cards, Mortgages, Deposits and a Design Centre of Excellence, four running at once. Every Tribe has its own building, its own product managers and its own Tribe Lead. The designers sit inside them. They still belong to one team.

I scale what works

I was the first designer in our studio to work in agile, before I was a manager. Once I was, I ran the Agile Guild that took it studio-wide. It sat on top of managing my designers and reached teams that were not mine.

Research is the first step, every time

A new requirement is where research starts, not where design starts. I check what we know, and when that is thin I ask for a customer journey study. For car loans, standing in dealerships and talking to salespeople and customers before anyone drew a screen.

Capabilities

Fluent in more than Figma.

Most design managers stop at the mockup. I don't, and in 2026, that gap is the whole point.

AI-native, not AI-curious

I build with Claude Code daily, prototyping flows, debugging logic, shipping real surfaces. For a design manager, that's not a hobby, it's a competitive edge.

Comfortable in the repo

Branches, diffs, pull requests, I read code the way I read Figma files. It closes the trust gap between design and engineering, for good.

Figma, past the basics

Variables, component APIs, the plugin and MCP ecosystem, I don't just design screens, I build the systems that let a team move fast.

Leadership impact

Beyond the work.

Mentorship that ships careers

I actively mentor junior and aspiring product designers, helping them navigate the industry, accelerate their growth, and land their first real role.

From classroom to production

Industry mentor for the Google & Reichman University UX grad project, built on a real Bank Hapoalim challenge. One graduate joined my team; their concept now lives in our credit card workflow.

Agile without the theater

A strong advocate for Agile, embedding designers into Dailies, Planning, Demos and PIP to align stakeholders fast and remove delivery friction, not add process for its own sake.

Teaching the path I walked

Guest lecturer, Academic College of Tel Aviv-Yaffo, "Psychology & UX." I share my path from junior designer to management, the learning curves and what actually sustains leadership.
